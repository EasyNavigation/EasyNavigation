Detailed Description

Turns the safety channel's SafetyStatus messages into what EasyNav applies.

With no valid status received within the last timeout seconds, the status is lost, and that is treated as a protective stop. Times are reception times on the monotonic clock, so they do not depend on the publisher's clock. received() and evaluate() may run in different threads.
